What is Data Science to Me?
 - Data Science to me means making sense of the world around me. Often times there's so much information out there and so much noise, that it is often times really difficulte to make sense of what is actually important or not in this world. At every point of the day, there are millions of people and ads fighting for our attention and trying to influence us. Data Science seeks to make sense of all of that by taking it all in and turning it into order and insight. It's clarity in a world that confuses us, and it's really what makes me happy when things happen to fall in there place when it seems like they wouldn't have at first.

- This previous project in our class really taught me a lot of what could potentially be expected of data scientist. It's not just some flashy job with a cool salary, but something that actually takes a lot of hardwork and understanting. Even with the age of AI, it taught me that although those tools are readily available to us. It's only pushed the bar higher for everyone, and that a deeper conceptual understanding of the matertial is necessary because we aren't just sitting around staring at code anymore, but instead starting to become the architects of our own design. That requires seeing a project from start to finish through an top down lens with stronger guidence for where something needs to go.
