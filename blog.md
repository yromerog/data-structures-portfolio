## [Blog 1: What is Data Science to Me?](blog/blog1.html)

Learning data science in a social and interdisciplinary context matters because while this is a career that comes with a lot of theory, much of the impactful work done in the world is through applying this field to disciplines that experience monumental benefits from its success. For example, financial products can be better tailored to consumers, or a bank's risk tolerance can be effectively measured. What I hope to learn from this course are effective methods for analyzing business needs and risks while being able to make sound decisions and recommendations that benefit the business and its stakeholders.
